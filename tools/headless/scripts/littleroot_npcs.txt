# Run after to_littleroot.txt (same ROM build): talk to the oddex test guide at (10,11)
# and the boy near (14,17). The boy wanders; his position is deterministic for identical
# inputs from boot, so rerun to_littleroot.txt first if the ROM changed.
load out/littleroot.ss
# (5,9) -> (10,9) -> (10,10), face the scientist below
hold RIGHT 80
hold DOWN 16
press DOWN 3 20
shot out/10_scientist.png
# A starts the talk; B advances/closes text and can never start a new interaction
press A 2 80
press B 2 40
press B 2 80
shot out/11_scientist_text_1.png
press B 2 40
press B 2 80
press B 2 40
wait 30
shot out/12_scientist_text_2.png
repeat 3 press B 2 30
wait 30
